FEL3250: Course project

Information and guidelines

  • Deadline for project proposal (title): February 18.

  • A project can be performed by up to two students.

  • A student can propose a project by emailing to anyone of the teachers, who will be the advisor.

  • A student can meet anyone of the teacher to ask for advices on defining a project. Typically, the student can just retell her/his ongoing research and the contacted teacher can suggest potential directions.

  • The project report consists in a paper in IEEE double column format of at least two-three pages containing: abstract, introduction with literature overview, problem formulation, solution roadmap with some important preliminary result. For preliminary results, minimally, the group should work out a small example which provides some intuition (or highlights a specific challenge) for the problem/method they are studying.

  • The project report is due on Thursday 6 March. (The deadline is extended to Thursday 13 March).

  • The projects are presented on Thursday 6 March at 10:00–12:00 in M37 and at 13:30–15:30 in M36.

  • Presentations should be of 10 minutes duration with an additional 5 minutes for questions and answers.

  • Marking is as follows: Presentation : 40%, Report : 60%
